Interesting Fact 1

In Spain, people eat 12 grapes as the clock strikes midnight. Each grape brings good luck for one month of the coming year.

Interesting Fact 2

In Greece, people smash a pomegranate against their front door at midnight for good luck.

Interesting Fact 3

In Estonia, citizens try to eat seven, nine, or twelve meals on New Year's Day to gain extra strength for the year.

Interesting Fact 4

In Colombia, citizens carry an empty suitcase and run around the block at midnight to have travel adventures in the new year.

Interesting Fact: In South Korea, families eat a traditional rice cake soup called tteokguk to gain long life and good health.

Interesting Fact: In South Africa, people throw old furniture out of their windows to start the year fresh.

Interesting Fact: In Argentina, citizens shred old documents and throw the paper scraps out the window like confetti to leave the past behind.

Interesting Fact: In the Netherlands, citizens eat delicious, deep-fried dough balls covered in powdered sugar called oliebollen.
